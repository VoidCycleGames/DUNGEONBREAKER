Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

VoidCycleGames ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game DUNGEONBREAKER (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Handle

The App stores gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, high scores, survival statistics, settings, virtual currency (gold), and unlocked upgrades.

The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions (for example, ad views and taps), and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Where required by applicable law, the App requests your consent before requesting personalized advertising. You may manage advertising-related privacy choices through the consent options presented in the App, where available.

Google Play Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google may process account, profile, and game-save information to provide sign-in, achievements, and cloud-save features.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • provide gameplay, save progress, and restore cloud saves when enabled;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • award in-game rewards after rewarded advertisements (such as free chests, rerolls, or revives);
  • prevent fraud and maintain the security and stability of the App.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. Information may be processed by service providers that support the App, including Google AdMob, Google Play Games, and Google User Messaging Platform, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it through the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Cloud-save and Google Play Games data are managed through your Google account and Google Play Games settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. Local data is protected with cryptographic checks and tamper detection. Data transmitted by Google services is handled using their applicable security protections.

Third-Party Links

Advertisements and external services may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
